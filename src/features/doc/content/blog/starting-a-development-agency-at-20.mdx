I started buildnboost in January 2019, in Mumbai, when I was 20. The first clients were in India, Dubai, and Oman. I took each project from design through deployment, mostly with Laravel, Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and Figma.

A few of those builds were Liplick Pizzeria on mobile and web, FlyCloudCross for a travel agency in Dubai, the Floret app for Mumbai University, and an event site with a CRM admin panel for Bombay College of Pharmacy.

In November 2021 I paused the agency and joined Nilede Technologies as a founding member and product lead. I owned product direction, sales, and a team of four. We shipped CRM backends, multi-tenant SaaS, and a pizza brand platform with a mobile app, a web app, and a franchise admin portal. Clients were in India, the US, and the UK.

I started buildnboost again in May 2023. It now also serves clients in the US, Israel, Dubai, and the UK, and I still run it alongside full-time work. When a build gets bigger than I should do alone, I outsource part of it. The offer is the same as it was at the start: design, development, and deployment under one roof.

Before the agency, from 2017 to 2018, I freelanced. SmartBistro was the first product where I owned both the design and the code. It was a cross-platform app built with Ionic, Angular, and Cordova.
